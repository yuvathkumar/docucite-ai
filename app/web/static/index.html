About DocuCite AI

Ask questions about your team's documents. Every answer shows where it came from.

What is it?

DocuCite AI is a question-and-answer tool for documents. Your team uploads its PDFs — policies, manuals, guides, reports — and anyone can ask a question in plain English. The answer comes with numbered citations that show the exact document, page and passage it is based on.

If the documents don't contain the answer, it says so. It does not guess.

Who is it for?

  • Employees and team members who need a quick, reliable answer from company documents without reading 80 pages.
  • New joiners learning the rules, tools and processes.
  • Document owners (HR, IT, security, operations, compliance, support) who want people to find answers themselves and want to see what people can't find.

What problem does it solve?

  • Knowledge is buried in long PDFs, so people ask colleagues or guess.
  • Normal search misses meaning: looking for “holiday” won't find a page that says “vacation”.
  • Generic AI chat tools can sound sure while inventing facts, with nothing to check.

How it works

  1. 1
    Upload. An admin adds a PDF. Its text is read page by page and split into short passages.
  2. 2
    Index. Each passage is stored for keyword search, and given a “meaning fingerprint” so related wording is found too.
  3. 3
    Ask. Your question is matched against the passages using both keywords and meaning, and the best ones are selected.
  4. 4
    Answer with sources. An AI model writes the answer using only those passages and cites them. If they don't contain the answer, you're told so.

What changes for your team

  • Faster answers — seconds instead of searching through documents.
  • Answers you can verify — every claim points to a page you can read.
  • Fewer repeat questions to the people who know the documents.
  • Visible gaps — admins can see which questions had no answer and add the missing documents.

Roles

  • User: asks questions and browses the document list.
  • Administrator: everything a user can do, plus uploading and deleting documents, managing users and reviewing the question log.

Every question and answer is logged for review by administrators.

Getting started (administrators)

  1. 1
    Open Documents and upload your PDFs. Wait until each one shows Ready.
  2. 2
    Open Users and create accounts for your team.
  3. 3
    Ask a test question you already know the answer to, and check the cited page.
  4. 4
    Review the Question log now and then. “Not found” entries show which documents are missing.

Good to know

  • AI can make mistakes. Always check the cited passage for anything important.
  • Answers are only as good and as current as the uploaded documents.
  • Only text-based PDFs work. Scanned pages (images) can't be read.
  • Privacy: document passages and questions are sent to the AI provider (Google Gemini). On its free tier they may be used to improve its products, so don't upload confidential documents.
